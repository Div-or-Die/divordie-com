Support

We answer our own email.

There is no ticket queue and no support bot. Messages go to the two people who built the software.

Contact

support@divordie.com

We aim to respond within two business days.

Before you write

Including a few details up front usually saves a round trip. Where it applies, please tell us:

  • The name of the application and its version number
  • Your device model and operating system version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • Whether the problem happens every time or only occasionally

Screenshots or a short screen recording are always welcome. Please do not send passwords or payment details — we will never ask for them.

Refunds and billing

Purchases made through the App Store, including in-app purchases, are billed and refunded by Apple rather than by us. Refund requests are handled through Apple’s Report a Problem page. If a purchase did not unlock correctly in one of our applications, write to us and we will help sort it out.

Bugs and feature requests

Both are genuinely useful to us and we read all of them. If you have hit a reproducible bug, the steps to reproduce it are the single most valuable thing you can send.

Privacy questions

For questions about what our software stores or transmits, see our privacy policy, or write to privacy@divordie.com.
