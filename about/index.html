About

Div or Die LLC is an independent game studio run by its two owners.

We formed the company to develop and publish our own games. Between the two of us we cover design, engineering, and art. Every project is built in-house from prototype to shipped build, and we choose the engine and stack per project rather than standardising on one.

We currently develop for iOS, Steam, and PC.

How we build

We work on a small number of projects at a time and release them when they are finished. Where the design allows it, our games store their data on the player's device and run without a network connection.

Our games contain no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. We do not collect personal information — see the privacy policy for details.
