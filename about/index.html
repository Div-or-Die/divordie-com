About

Div or Die LLC is an independent game studio owned and run by a husband-and-wife team.

We started the company to build games on our own terms — the ones we kept sketching out and wanting to play, without waiting for permission from anyone else to make them.

Between the two of us we cover design, engineering, art direction, and every unglamorous thing a company requires. Every project is built in-house from the first prototype to the shipped build. We work across iOS, Steam, and PC, in engines and stacks chosen per project rather than by habit.

What we believe

Feel before features

The core interaction has to be fun before anything gets layered on top. If the moment-to-moment isn’t good with placeholder art and no score, more content won’t rescue it.

Ship it finished

We’d rather release one game that’s genuinely done than three that are nearly there. That’s why we talk about what we’ve built more than what we intend to build.

Respect the player

No ads. No dark patterns. No data collection we can’t justify. Our games keep their data on the player’s device wherever the design allows it, and they work with the network switched off.

Small on purpose

Two people is a constraint, and constraints are useful. It keeps the scope honest, the decisions fast, and the person who made a thing reachable when you have a question about it.
