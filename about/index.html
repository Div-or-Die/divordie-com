About

A two-person studio.

Div or Die LLC is an independent software company owned and operated by a husband-and-wife team.

We started the company to build the kind of software we wanted to use and could not find: applications that do one thing carefully, run without a network connection, and do not treat the person using them as a data source.

Between us we cover design, engineering, and everything else a small company requires. Every product we release is built in-house from the first sketch to the shipped binary. We work across iOS, macOS, and PC.

What we make

Our catalogue spans games and practical tools. The common thread is craft: we would rather ship one considered product than three unfinished ones. Titles are announced when they are ready for people to use, and each released product gets its own support and privacy documentation on this site.
