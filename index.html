What we’re building

We work across genres rather than picking a lane. Right now that means a handful of projects at very different stages, from prototypes to titles preparing for release.

  • Mobile

    Ambient simulation

    Living, procedurally animated worlds you can sit with. Rendered in real time, built to run smoothly and stay out of your way.

  • Desktop

    Puzzle games

    Systems-driven puzzles with a hard edge and a terminal streak. Small rule sets, deep consequences.

  • PC & Console

    Arcade sports

    Physics-first sports built around feel. If the ball isn’t fun before there’s a scoreboard, nothing else matters.

  • Live

    Interactive streaming

    Games that turn a live audience into the controller, where the chat is a player rather than a spectator.

How we work

Everything is built in-house — design, code, and art, by the two of us. No publishers, no outsourcing, no roadmap written by someone who has never played the build.

We care about the parts players feel and studios usually cut: how a control responds, whether a game runs at full frame rate on hardware people actually own, whether it works with the network switched off. Our games keep their data on your device. We don’t build ads into them and we don’t collect information we have no use for.

We announce things when they’re real. Titles get their own pages here as they approach release, along with whatever support and documentation they need.
