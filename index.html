DIV.OR.DIE

A two-person independent game studio building games for iOS, Steam, and PC.

What we're building

We work across several genres. Projects are at different stages, from prototypes to titles preparing for release.

  • // mobile

    Ambient simulation

    Real-time animated environments, rendered on-device.

  • // desktop

    Puzzle games

    Grid and tile puzzles with small rule sets and long solve chains.

  • // pc + console

    Arcade sports

    Physics-driven arcade sports, built around ball handling and control.

  • // live

    Interactive streaming

    Games driven by live stream events, where the audience provides input.

How we work

Design, code, and art are handled in-house by the two of us. We work without a publisher.

Our games store their data on the player's device and run without a network connection. They contain no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.

Individual titles get their own pages here as they approach release, along with the support and privacy documentation each one requires.
