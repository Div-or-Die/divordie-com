Independent Software Studio

We build software worth finishing.

Div or Die is a small, independent studio making games and applications for Apple platforms and the desktop.

We are a two-person company. We design, build, and ship our own products rather than taking them through committees. That means we get to care about the parts that usually get cut — how an app feels in one hand, whether it works without a network, whether it respects the person using it.

How we work

We favor software that is local-first, self-contained, and honest about what it does. Our applications keep your data on your device wherever the product allows it. We do not build advertising into our software, and we do not collect information we have no use for.

We ship deliberately. Products are released when they are genuinely finished, which is why we would rather talk about what we have built than what we intend to build.

Current work

We have several titles in active development for iOS, macOS, and PC. Released products will be listed here, along with their support and privacy documentation, as they become available.
