Privacy Policy

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. Our games are built to work without accounts and without a network connection.

Effective 20 August 2026. Applies to Div or Die LLC and all software we publish.

Information we collect

None. Div or Die LLC does not operate servers that receive personal information from our games. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address to use our software.

Content you create in our games — including text you enter, photos you add, settings you choose, and your progress or game state — is written to storage on your own device. It is not transmitted to us and we have no ability to read it.

Analytics and advertising

Our games contain no analytics frameworks, no advertising software development kits, no third-party trackers, and no crash-reporting services that transmit data to us. We do not use cookies or tracking technologies on this website.

Device backups and iCloud

Because application data is stored on your device, it may be included in backups you choose to make through Apple’s iCloud or a local computer backup. Those backups are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and are controlled entirely by you through your device settings. We have no access to them.

Purchases

Where our games offer paid downloads or in-app purchases, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details; we never see or store your payment information. Our software receives only a confirmation from the operating system that a purchase is valid, which is used to unlock the corresponding features on your device.

Children

Our games do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, delete, or transfer.

Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete personal information a company holds about you. We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to return or erase.

You remain in full control of the data our games store on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing the content within the game or by deleting the game itself, which removes its stored data from your device.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to put in that message, including your email address. We use it only to reply to you. We do not add correspondents to mailing lists and we do not share these messages with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we release software that works differently — for example, a product with an optional online feature — we will update this policy before that software is released and revise the effective date above. Material changes will be described on this page.
